## Theme Colors

trigr includes a full dark theme with customizable accent color. You can tailor the look to match your workflow.

## Ender Character

The ender character is the key that triggers expansion after typing an abbreviation. By default, trigr expands after common punctuation and whitespace. This can be configured to suit your typing style.

## System Tray

trigr runs in the Windows system tray. Right-click the tray icon to open the Trigger Manager or manage the app.
